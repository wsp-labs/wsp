stdio.open runs a command under bash -c in the daemon's root, in a process group of its own, with its stdin, stdout and stderr as pipes held for the socket that opened it; stdio.write feeds its stdin, stdout and stderr come back apart as stdio.out and stdio.err, base64 and unchanged, and stdio.close or the socket's close kills the group and says stdio.exit; any of the three that names a machine is refused, so a command a machine's config chose never runs on the computer outside it.
