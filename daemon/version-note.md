A daemon started with --doors, which the host's unit for a provider's machine passes, serves two doors on that machine itself: the HTTP door on 127.0.0.1:7071 and [::1]:7071, and the gh door on /root/.wsp/broker.sock, which writes /root/.wsp/gh/config.yml naming itself as it opens. A daemon started without it serves no door and answers a pipe auth as a control socket, as before. On one with the doors, a socket whose auth frame says pipe is parked, at most 64 at once, and hears nothing until a connection on either door takes it: then one pipe.open frame naming the door and how long the connection waited, and the connection's bytes both ways as binary frames until either end closes. With no pipe parked a connection is held unread for at most an hour, dropped as soon as its client is gone, and never sent twice: a pipe that drops closes its connection at once. git.push, git.startOn, git.update, git.mergeIn, git.pr and git.prCheckout with a token run their git, where the HTTP door is bound, with that door as the proxy for github.com and the token as its password, and their gh with the token as GH_TOKEN through the gh door.
